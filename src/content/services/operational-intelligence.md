The work between your own teams is often the least visible and the most manual. An approval waits in an inbox. A number gets reconciled by hand every month. A handoff depends on a person forwarding a thread. Internal Operations makes those steps run on their own and surfaces the ones that are stuck.

## The symptoms it addresses

- Approvals sit for days because no one can see what is waiting on them.
- The same reconciliation is done by hand every close, copying figures between tabs.
- Work handed from one team to another loses context and has to be reassembled.

## What we deliver

- Approval routing that shows who owns a decision and how long it has been waiting.
- Reconciliation and reporting assembled from the source systems, not rebuilt by hand.
- Structured handoffs that carry the context each team needs to pick the work up cleanly.

## Where a human stays in the loop

Approvals stay with the people accountable for them — the system routes and reminds, but it does not decide. Exceptions in a reconciliation are flagged for a person to investigate rather than smoothed over.
